## Fix direction

Extract shared helpers and keep a single source of truth for each behavior.

## Fix

All 8 items resolved and machine-verified (`pytest`, `mypy .`, `ruff check .`):

1–2. Unified `WindowArrangement.hsplit`/`vsplit` via `_split`, buffer cycling via `go_to_next_buffer(_step)`, and tab cycling via `_cycle_tab(step)` in `vith/window_arrangement.py`.
3 & 8. Extracted `_visual_block_ordinal_bounds`, `_verify_signature`, and `_apply_patch` in `vith/thai/patches.py` to eliminate duplicated visual-block rectangle math and signature/patch-registration boilerplate.
4. Added `_map_column_across_lines` in `vith/thai/clusters.py` and cached `(src_col, src_row, ordinal)` in `ColdTextBuffer._cursor_vertical` (`vith/cold_text.py`) so repeated vertical moves reuse the resolved ordinal.
5. Extracted `press_enter_to_continue()` in `vith/commands/commands.py` and shared it with `vith/rc_file.py`.
6. Centralized `_expand_location(location)` in `vith/io/backends.py` and delegated `GZipFileIO.can_open_location` to `super().can_open_location`.
7. Unified streaming and legacy per-location lexer caching in `DocumentLexer._ensure_lexer` (`vith/lexer.py`).
