# `COMMAND_GRAMMAR` rejects spaces in file/buffer paths and punctuation trailing args

**Category:** commands / grammar · **Area:** `vith/commands/grammar.py`, `vith/commands/completer.py` · **Found at:** Round 18 full-project audit (2026-11-24, `v0.16.5`, hg rev `458:d2d313328875`) · **Fix status:** fixed (hg rev 473)

## Summary

`COMMAND_GRAMMAR` restricts file locations and buffer selectors to non-whitespace tokens (`(?P<location>[^\s]+)`, `(?P<write_append>[^\s]+)`, `(?P<buffer_name>[^\s]+)`), and restricts `:d`/`:y`/`:j`/`:pu` arguments to `[0-9a-zA-Z"]+`:[^src-grammar]

1. **Paths and buffer names with spaces fail:** Both `_EnvPathCompleter` and `BufferNameCompleter` yield unescaped paths containing spaces (e.g. `/tmp/my file.py`).[^src-comp] Pressing `<Enter>` on `:e /tmp/my file.py`, `:w /tmp/my file.py`, `:cd My Documents`, or `:b /tmp/my file.py` fails `COMMAND_GRAMMAR.match(...)` and reports `Unknown command: ...`. Even escaped `\ ` (`:e my\ file.py`) is rejected because `[^\s]+` stops at the space.
2. **Invalid punctuation args to `:d`/`:y`/`:j`/`:pu` bypass `E488`:** Typing `:d @` or `:j -1` fails `(?P<cmd_arg>[0-9a-zA-Z"]+)` and falls out of `COMMAND_GRAMMAR` entirely, reporting `Unknown command: d @` instead of `E488: Trailing characters` (unlike `:sort @`, whose grammar uses `(?P<cmd_arg>[^\s]+)`).

## Evidence

Live probe against `handle_command`:

* Open `"/tmp/.../my file.py"` via `load_initial_files`, then `handle_command(ed, f"b {spaced}")` → `ed.message == "Unknown command: b /tmp/.../my file.py"`.
* `handle_command(ed, "d @")` → `ed.message == "Unknown command: d @"`.

## Fix Direction

1. Widen single-argument file/buffer rules (`location`, `write_append`, `buffer_name`) to accept the trimmed argument tail (`\S.*`, unescaping `\ ` or stripping surrounding quotes where appropriate) so paths with spaces round-trip cleanly from Tab completion and manual entry.
2. Widen `:d`/`:y`/`:j`/`:pu`'s `cmd_arg` pattern from `[0-9a-zA-Z"]+` to `\S.*` (matching `:sort`/`:retab`) so `_register_name` and `join_lines` consistently emit `E488: Trailing characters`.
3. Add regression tests for `:e`, `:w`, `:cd`, and `:b` on paths containing spaces, and `:d @` / `:j -1` reporting `E488`.
