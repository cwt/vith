## Fix

All 24 items resolved and machine-verified (`pytest`, `mypy .`, `ruff check .`):

1. Moved `_cell_ordinal`, `_start_of_cell_ordinal`, and `_map_column_across_lines` to `vith/thai/clusters.py` and replaced the duplicated module docstring in `vith/thai/patches.py`.
2. Reordered `_THAI_DIGIT_CPS`, `_THAI_SYMBOL_CPS`, `_build_thai_base_table()`, and `_THAI_BASE_CPS` above `is_thai_base` in `vith/thai/classify.py`.
3–4. Replaced `already_Hidden = [False]` with `nonlocal already_hidden` in `WelcomeMessageWindow` and guarded `active_tab is None` and unknown node types in `EditorLayout.update()` (`vith/layout.py`).
5. Added explicit `return None` statements in `_get_split_parent`, `active_editor_buffer`, `active_pt_window`, and `get_editor_buffer_for_location` (`vith/window_arrangement.py`).
6–7. Clarified `PRIMARY_ENCODING`/`FALLBACK_ENCODING`/`ENCODINGS` and `_auto_decode` in `vith/io/backends.py` and aligned `write(..., encoding='utf-8')` signatures across all `EditorIO` subclasses.
8–10. Updated `run_rc_file` in `vith/rc_file.py` to accept `os.PathLike`, open with `encoding='utf-8'`, and share `press_enter_to_continue()` (with `EOFError`/`KeyboardInterrupt` guards) with `vith/commands/commands.py`.
11. Sliced Linux `PR_SET_NAME` process title after UTF-8 encoding (`name.encode('utf-8')[:15]`) in `vith/process.py`.
12–13. Expanded config paths before legacy check and used `os.makedirs(..., exist_ok=True)` in `vith/editor.py`.
14–15. Initialized `_last_text_len`/`_last_line_count`/`_has_unsaved_changes` before `ColdTextBuffer` construction and replaced `for...else` backend lookups with `next((...), None)` in `vith/editor_buffer.py`.
16. Added type annotations to `DocumentLexer.__init__` and `lex_document` (`vith/lexer.py`).
17–18. Added `_LazyStylesDict.__contains__`, pre-compile style validation, and documentation for the `'vim'` scheme override in `vith/style.py`.
19–20. Moved all `Condition` definitions above key-binding registrations and clarified `disable_default_transpose_chars` (`filter=~in_insert_mode`) in `vith/key_bindings.py`.
21. Documented and typed the size-guarded `_word_spans_for_line` cache wrapper in `vith/thai/words.py`.
22 & 24. Updated the stray-mark comment, simplified `_verify_signature` parameter checks, and optimized the SARA AM column loop in `vith/thai/patches.py`.
23. Aligned `BufferNameCompleter` and `ColorSchemeCompleter` in `vith/commands/completer.py`.
