## Fix Direction

1. Remove dead helpers/branches/unused parameters (`#1`–`#10`), migrating any unit tests that import superseded internal helpers (`_visual_block_ordinal_bounds`, `_end_of_visual_col`, `_cell_has_sara_am`, `check_width`) onto the live functions (`_get_visual_block_bounds`, `_visual_block_row_span`, `_sanitize_and_invalidate_sara_am`, `on_after_render`), and fix the misplaced docstring in `_compute_cell_columns` plus stale comments (`#11`).
2. Extract `_exec_editor_script(editor, path, content, label, error_handler)` in `vith/rc_file.py` (`#12`).
3. Share `_canonical_filetype` (with validation option for unknown names) across `editor.py`, `lexer.py`, `options.py`, and `preview.py` (`#13`).
4. Factor a helper method on `ColdTextBuffer` for `_invalidate_edit_tracking()` and known-edit mutation recording (`#14`), and reuse `is_buffer_read_only` and `in_insert_mode` (`#15`).
